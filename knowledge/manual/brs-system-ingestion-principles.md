# BRS system ingestion principles

The BRS system crawler is used to learn reusable product behaviour, not to copy a club's private setup.

Approved knowledge includes menu structure, page purpose, setting names, product-authored help text, workflow steps, report names, filters, and relationships between areas such as tee sheet, payments, competitions, memberships, reports, and system configuration.

Do not store member names, visitor names, staff names, email addresses, phone numbers, booking references, payment amounts, green fee values, transaction identifiers, custom club policy text, or exact audit timestamps. When audit metadata is useful, record only that the product exposes last-modified information.

System knowledge must be treated as template knowledge unless it has been reviewed and approved as broadly applicable product guidance.
