# BRS Payments And Competition Reconciliation Case Evidence

Use this approved support evidence when a customer asks about booking payment status, outstanding booking balances, payouts, refunds, open competition/tournament reconciliation, debit card entries, competition purse, or insufficient funds.

## Booking says outstanding but customer says card payment was taken

Do not rely on the Timesheet balance alone. Check the booking and the payment records together:

1. Open the Timesheet for the booking date/course.
2. Open the tee time or booking details for the booking reference.
3. Check the booking Payments section for balance, amount, method, payment status, and refund status.
4. Open Tools -> BRS Payments -> Transactions.
5. Search/filter by booking reference, date, customer name, and amount where available.
6. Check whether the transaction is completed, failed, pending, refunded, or missing.
7. Open Tools -> BRS Payments -> Payouts to check whether a completed transaction appears in a payout schedule.
8. Use Tools -> BRS Payments -> Refunds only after the original transaction and refund route are confirmed.

If no completed BRS Payments transaction is found, explain that BRS cannot confirm funds or process a BRS refund from that booking until the successful transaction is found. Escalate with booking reference, booking date/time, customer/payment details, transaction search result, and payout evidence.

## Member competition purse insufficient funds

For member competition purse or wallet issues, check the competition record and system purse settings before changing charges:

1. Confirm the active competition record is the one members are entering, especially when a cancelled or duplicate competition has the same name.
2. Check the entry fee.
3. Check the charge method: Competition Purse or Competition Wallet.
4. Check member entry status.
5. Check Competition Purse Transactions or member competition purse history for pending or already processed charges.
6. Open System Configuration and check the Competition Purse Module.
7. Check Minimum member competition purse balance.

If the entry fee would take the member below the configured minimum purse balance, BRS can block entry even when the visible purse balance is above the entry fee. Phrase this as a check, not a confirmed cause, unless the club's live records prove it.

Process Competition Charges is for competitions set up to use Competition Purse or Competition Wallet and should be used after the competition is over or closed.

## Open competition or tournament payment reconciliation

Open competitions and tournaments often use online/card payments or payment links rather than member competition purse. The tee sheet or start sheet alone may exclude withdrawn players, so it cannot prove all paid entrants.

Use these evidence sources together:

1. Competition entry list, including entered, withdrawn, cancelled, or removed entries where the competition page exposes those statuses.
2. Tools -> BRS Payments -> Transactions filtered by payment date, playing/competition date, reference, customer, amount, or description.
3. Reports such as Revenue From Online Merchant Payments.
4. Payment date or transaction date reports where available.
5. Refunds report or BRS Payments -> Refunds when reconciling no-refund or partial-refund cases.

Compare exported payment totals with the entry and withdrawal lists. Escalate when transaction exports and competition entries still do not reconcile.
