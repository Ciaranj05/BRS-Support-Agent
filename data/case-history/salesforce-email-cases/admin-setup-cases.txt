# ADMIN SETUP – SALESFORCE CASE HISTORY

## CASE: buggy-availability-not-matching

Topic: admin-setup
Product area: buggy booking / availability

Customer question:
The number of available buggies on the tee sheet does not match the configured amount.

Resolved guidance:
- Confirm the number of buggies configured.
- Confirm the before/after reservation time for buggy usage.
- Check whether existing tee times already have buggy allocations.
- Confirm visitors are allowed to book buggies online.
- Click Update after changes.
- Verify the tee sheet availability updates correctly.

Use when user asks:
- buggy availability incorrect
- buggy count wrong
- buggy numbers not updating
- buggy booking issue

Do not use if:
- Approved buggy guidance already answers the question directly.
