# PAYMENTS – SALESFORCE CASE HISTORY

## CASE: missing-top-up-not-showing

Topic: payments
Product area: memberships / wallets

Customer question:
Member says a top-up payment is missing.

Resolved guidance:
- Check Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions.
- Search the date range for the payment.
- Check Memberships >> Reports >> Wallet Transactions.
- Confirm the wallet is assigned to the member.
- If the transaction does not exist in BRS Payments, escalate to support.

Use when user asks:
- missing top-up
- wallet credit not showing
- member balance incorrect
- payment missing

Do not use if:
- Approved knowledge contains a direct answer that conflicts with this case.
