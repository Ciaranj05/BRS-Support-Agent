# MEMBERSHIPS – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

---

## Access Memberships

Go to:
Memberships

---

## Add a member

1. Go to: Memberships >> Members
2. Click Create Member
3. Enter member details
4. Click Save

---

## Edit a member

1. Go to: Memberships >> Members
2. Search for the member
3. Click Edit
4. Update the details
5. Click Save

---

## Create a subscription

1. Go to: Memberships >> Subscriptions
2. Click Create Subscription
3. Enter the details
4. Click Save

Important:
- Subscriptions must be applied before billing.
- Subscriptions can be Membership or Fee.
- Pricing can be Fixed or By Membership Type.
- If pricing is missing, the system may flag an error.
- Pro-rata subscriptions are used when members join mid-cycle.

---

## Apply a subscription

1. Go to: Memberships >> Subscriptions
2. Click Apply / Remove Subscription
3. Select the subscription
4. Choose members, membership types, or filters
5. Preview and confirm

---

## Remove a subscription

1. Go to: Memberships >> Subscriptions
2. Click Apply / Remove Subscription
3. Select Remove
4. Choose the subscription
5. Preview and confirm

Important:
- Subscriptions cannot be removed if linked to an active bill.

---

## Create a bill

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Click Create Bills
3. Enter billing details
4. Select who to bill
5. Select subscriptions or items
6. Preview
7. Confirm

Important:
- Bills are created as drafts until published.
- Bills must be published before members can see or pay them.

---

## Publish a bill

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Find the bill
3. Click the bill reference
4. Click Publish

---

## Resend a bill

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Click the bill reference
3. Click Resend Bill

---

## Edit a bill

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Find the bill
3. Click the bill reference
4. Click Edit
5. Make the required changes
6. Save
7. Resend or republish if required

Important:
- Bills can be edited after publishing.
- If payment exists, restrictions may apply.
- You may need to refund before making some changes.

---

## Cancel or delete a bill

- Draft bill → can be deleted
- Published bill → must be cancelled

Important:
- Cancelled bills remain visible and may contain payment history.

---

## Take a payment

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Find the bill
3. Click Make Payment
4. Enter payment details
5. Confirm

---

## Payment allocation

Payments may exist but not be allocated correctly.

To check:

1. Open member profile
2. Go to Billing tab
3. Open the bill
4. Review payment allocation

Important:
- If payment exists but bill still shows outstanding, check allocation first.

---

## Refund a payment

1. Find the payment on the bill
2. Click Refund
3. Confirm

Important:
- Online refunds go back to the card.
- Offline refunds must be completed outside the system.
- Refunds should be processed against the correct payment.

---

## Payment schemes

Payment schemes allow members to pay by instalments.

Important:
- Payment schemes must total 100%.
- Payment schemes can be Even or Custom.
- Payment schemes must be correctly configured before applying to bills.

To check payment schemes:

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Payment Schemes
2. Check payment days
3. Check instalment type
4. Confirm distribution totals 100%

---

## Scheduled payment issues

Check:

1. Member is active
2. Payment scheme setup
3. Saved card details
4. Failed scheduled payments

Important:
- Scheduled payments are attempted daily.
- Failed scheduled payments retry up to 5 times.
- Inactive members cannot log in and scheduled payments will not process.

---

## Member cannot see bill

Check:

1. The bill has been published
2. The member email is correct
3. Resend the bill

Important:
- Unpublished bill → member cannot see it.

---

## Bill not matching expectation

Ask:
“What looks incorrect — amount too high, missing payment, or extra charge?”

Then check:

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Open the bill
3. Review subscriptions applied
4. Review custom charges
5. Review discounts
6. Check payment allocation if payment is involved

---

## Bill too high

Check:

1. Go to: Memberships >> Billing / Payments
2. Open the bill
3. Review subscriptions applied
4. Review custom charges
5. Review discounts

---

## Missing payment on bill

1. Open member profile
2. Go to Billing tab
3. Open the bill
4. Review payment allocation
5. Check Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions if needed

Important:
- Payment may exist but not be allocated correctly.
- If no record exists in BRS but money has been received, escalate.

---

## Reports

Go to:
Memberships >> Reports

Reports include:
- Bill history
- Revenue projections
- Payment tracking
- Membership retention

---

## Wallets

Wallets must be assigned before use.

Types:
- Points wallets
- Currency wallets

Used for:
- Flexible Membership
- Saving towards bills

To check wallet settings:

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Wallets
2. Check wallet is active
3. Confirm wallet is assigned to the member
4. Check top-up options

Important:
- Points wallets are used for Flexible Membership.
- Wallets do not automatically pay bills except where supported by specific setup.

---

## Chained memberships

Chained memberships move members between membership types automatically.

Used for:
- Age-based changes
- Length of service changes

Requires:
- Date of birth
- Join date

---

## Member filters

Member filters are used for:
- Applying subscriptions
- Removing subscriptions
- Creating bills
- Reporting

To create a member filter:

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Member Filters
2. Click Create Member Filter
3. Add description and subtitle
4. Add conditions
5. Save the filter

# MEMBERSHIPS SETTINGS LOGIC

---

## Core rule

When a user asks about Memberships settings, do not list all settings.

First ask:
“What are you trying to do or fix within Memberships settings?”

Then route them to the correct area.

---

## General settings

Use when the issue relates to:
- Members being blocked
- Members inactive
- Members unable to log in
- Members unable to book
- Grace periods
- Failed scheduled payments

Go to:
Memberships >> Settings >> General

Check:
- Grace Period
- Auto deactivation rules
- Scheduled payments grace period

Context:
Overdue bills or failed scheduled payments can automatically deactivate members.

---

## Payment Schemes

Use when the issue relates to:
- Payment plans
- Instalments
- Monthly payments
- Split payments

Go to:
Memberships >> Settings >> Payment Schemes

Check:
- Payment days
- Instalment type
- Distribution totals 100%
- Surcharge rules

Context:
Payment schemes control how members can spread payments.

---

## Tax Settings

Use when the issue relates to:
- VAT
- Tax
- Incorrect billing amounts

Go to:
Memberships >> Settings >> Tax Settings

Context:
Tax rates are applied to subscriptions.

Also check:
Memberships >> Subscriptions

---

## Subscription Cycles

Use when the issue relates to:
- Subscription renewal
- Annual renewal
- Monthly renewal
- Rolling membership

Go to:
Memberships >> Settings >> Subscription Cycles

Context:
Subscription cycles control the period a subscription applies to.

Also check:
Memberships >> Subscriptions

## Payment Types

- Missing payment methods

Memberships >> Settings >> Payment Types

## Membership Statuses

## Note Types

# COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING

---

## Why has a member become inactive?

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> General
2. Check Grace Period settings
3. Check automatic deactivation settings
4. Check if scheduled payments have failed

Important:
Members can be automatically deactivated if bills are overdue or scheduled payments fail.

Follow-up:
Is this affecting one member or multiple members?

---

## Why can’t a member log in or book?

1. Check the member’s status
2. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> General
3. Check grace period and auto-deactivation settings
4. Check whether scheduled payments have failed

Follow-up:
Is the member showing as Active or Inactive?

---

## Why is a payment scheme not working?

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Payment Schemes
2. Check payment days
3. Check instalment type
4. Confirm payment distribution totals 100%

Follow-up:
Is this payment scheme already applied to a bill?

---

## Why is a subscription not renewing?

1. Go to: Memberships >> Subscriptions
2. Open the subscription
3. Check whether Renews Automatically is enabled
4. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Subscription Cycles
5. Confirm the correct cycle exists

Follow-up:
Is the subscription set to renew automatically?

---

## Why is VAT or tax wrong on a bill?

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Tax Settings
2. Check the tax rate
3. Go to: Memberships >> Subscriptions
4. Check the tax rate applied to the subscription

Important:
Tax is applied through the subscription setup.

Follow-up:
Is the issue affecting one subscription or multiple subscriptions?

---

## Why can’t a member top up their wallet?

1. Go to: Memberships >> Settings >> Wallets
2. Check the wallet is active
3. Confirm the wallet is assigned to the member
4. Check top-up options

Follow-up:
Is this a points wallet or a currency wallet?

---

# CAUSE → EFFECT

- Inactive member → payments may fail
- Unpublished bill → member cannot see it
- Failed scheduled payments → member may become inactive
- Missing subscription → bill may be missing expected charge
- Payment exists but not allocated → bill may still show outstanding
- Subscription not applied → bill cannot be created correctly
- Subscription linked to active bill → subscription cannot be removed

---

# ESCALATION TRIGGERS

Escalate if:
- Settings appear correct but the system behaves differently
- Multiple members are affected unexpectedly
- Members are being deactivated incorrectly
- Payments or scheduled payments are failing repeatedly
- Billing inconsistencies
- Subscription errors
- Payment mismatches
- Member disputes
- No payment exists in BRS but money has been received
- The issue is not covered in uploaded knowledge
