# MEMBERSHIPS – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

Use this knowledge for members, bills, subscriptions, payment schemes, wallets, membership payments, member access, and billing queries.

---

## CORE RULE

For membership/billing issues, check the member profile and bill before assuming a payment or system issue.

Primary checks:

1. Member profile
2. Billing area
3. Relevant bill
4. Payments/allocation
5. Subscription/payment scheme

---

## MEMBER PROFILE

Use the member profile to check:

- Member name
- Membership status
- Active/inactive status
- Contact details
- Billing records
- Payment schemes
- Wallet/balance
- Subscriptions

If a member cannot be found, confirm:

- Name
- Email
- Membership number if available

## APPROVED ANSWER: create-member-profile-account
Member booking accounts are handled through member registration and the member profile, not through a visitor account route.

Use this route when the user has clarified they mean a member profile/account:

1. Give the member the club's BRS Members Tee Time Reservation URL, or add that URL to the club website.
2. The member registers their details through the club website/member booking link.
3. The club verifies the registration.
4. Enable the member's profile once the registration has been verified.
5. After the profile is enabled, the member can sign in and make bookings.

If the club uses a Club Systems website with Single Sign On, the member should log in through the Members area of the Club Systems website instead. Contact Club Systems if Single Sign On, Diary Integration, or BRS booking links need to be enabled.

Source: [Member Registration](https://help.brsgolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005511873-Member-Registration)
## END APPROVED ANSWER

## BILLING

Use Billing to check:

- Bills
- Charges
- Payments
- Discounts
- Subscriptions
- Outstanding balance
- Published/unpublished status

If a bill looks wrong, ask:

“What looks incorrect — amount too high, missing payment, or extra charge?”

---

## BILL TOO HIGH

Check:

1. Subscriptions applied
2. Custom charges
3. Discounts
4. Previous unpaid balance
5. Payment allocation

If the total still does not match expectation, escalate.

---

## BILL NOT VISIBLE

Check:

1. Bill exists
2. Bill is published
3. Correct member profile is selected
4. Member has access

If published bill is still not visible, escalate.

---

## PAYMENT NOT APPLIED TO BILL

If payment exists but bill still shows unpaid:

1. Open member profile
2. Go to Billing
3. Open the bill
4. Check payments
5. Check allocation
6. Confirm whether payment is applied to the correct bill

Important:

- Payment may exist but be unallocated
- Do not assume payment is missing until allocation is checked

If payment is missing entirely, use payments knowledge.

---

## SUBSCRIPTIONS

Use subscriptions to check recurring or membership charges.

Check:

- Subscription type
- Amount
- Start date
- End date
- Frequency
- Member status
- Applied discounts

### SUBSCRIPTION CYCLE TYPES

If asked “What subscription cycle types are available?” respond:

“There are subscription cycle options including Annual, Rolling, Monthly, and Custom.”

If subscription amount is wrong, check the subscription setup before editing the bill.

---

## PAYMENT SCHEMES

Use payment schemes to manage scheduled membership payments.

Check:

- Scheme start date
- Payment frequency
- Amount
- Member status
- Failed payments
- Linked bill

If asked “Scheduled payment has not been taken on the scheduled date” respond:

“Check if the member status was active or inactive in advance of the scheduled payment. Scheduled payments will only continue to be taken against members with an Active status.

If the member is active, then the payment attempt may have failed. The Payment scheduler will try again for 5 consecutive nights, so check again tomorrow.

If the payment is not taken, please contact support and provide them with information including the members name, bill name, and scheduled payment date.”

If payment scheme fails:

1. Check member status
2. Check payment method
3. Check failed payment reason
4. Escalate if repeated or affecting multiple members

## ONLINE PAYMENTS & PAYMENTS PLATFORM

### WHAT IS THE BRS PAYMENTS PLATFORM?

If asked “What is the BRS Payments platform?” respond:

“It is the integrated online payments solution used for membership bills, wallet top-ups, visitor fees, and other BRS payment services.”

### HOW DO I SET UP ONLINE PAYMENTS FOR MY CLUB?

If asked “How do I set up online payments for my club?” respond:

“Contact BRS support or your onboarding contact to configure your Payments account.”

### CAN MEMBERS PAY BILLS ONLINE?

If asked “Can members pay bills online?” respond:

“Yes, members can pay bills online through the member portal or BRS Members booking app.”

### CAN ADMIN USERS TAKE PAYMENTS THROUGH THE SYSTEM?

If asked “Can admin users take payments through the system?” respond:

“Yes, admins can process manual and online payments on behalf of members.”

### WHY ARE SCHEDULED PAYMENTS NOT RETRYING AFTER FAILURE?

If asked “Why are scheduled payments not retrying after failure?” respond:

“Scheduled payment retries only occur if the Scheduled Payments Grace Period is enabled.”

### WHAT IS THE MINIMUM SCHEDULED PAYMENT GRACE PERIOD?

If asked “What is the minimum scheduled payment grace period?” respond:

“The minimum is 6 days because the system retries failed payments for 5 consecutive days.”
