# MEMBERSHIPS – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

Use this knowledge for members, bills, subscriptions, payment schemes, wallets, membership payments, member access, and billing queries.

---

## CORE RULE

For membership/billing issues, check the member profile and bill before assuming a payment or system issue.

Primary checks:

1. Member profile
2. Billing area
3. Relevant bill
4. Payments/allocation
5. Subscription/payment scheme

---

## MEMBER PROFILE

Use the member profile to check:

- Member name
- Membership status
- Active/inactive status
- Contact details
- Billing records
- Payment schemes
- Wallet/balance
- Subscriptions

If a member cannot be found, confirm:

- Name
- Email
- Membership number if available

---

## BILLING

Use Billing to check:

- Bills
- Charges
- Payments
- Discounts
- Subscriptions
- Outstanding balance
- Published/unpublished status

If a bill looks wrong, ask:

“What looks incorrect — amount too high, missing payment, or extra charge?”

---

## BILL TOO HIGH

Check:

1. Subscriptions applied
2. Custom charges
3. Discounts
4. Previous unpaid balance
5. Payment allocation

If the total still does not match expectation, escalate.

---

## BILL NOT VISIBLE

Check:

1. Bill exists
2. Bill is published
3. Correct member profile is selected
4. Member has access

If published bill is still not visible, escalate.

---

## PAYMENT NOT APPLIED TO BILL

If payment exists but bill still shows unpaid:

1. Open member profile
2. Go to Billing
3. Open the bill
4. Check payments
5. Check allocation
6. Confirm whether payment is applied to the correct bill

Important:

- Payment may exist but be unallocated
- Do not assume payment is missing until allocation is checked

If payment is missing entirely, use payments knowledge.

---

## SUBSCRIPTIONS

Use subscriptions to check recurring or membership charges.

Check:

- Subscription type
- Amount
- Start date
- End date
- Frequency
- Member status
- Applied discounts

If subscription amount is wrong, check the subscription setup before editing the bill.

---

## PAYMENT SCHEMES

Use payment schemes to manage scheduled membership payments.

Check:

- Scheme start date
- Payment frequency
- Amount
- Member status
- Failed payments
- Linked bill

If payment scheme fails:

1. Check member status
2. Check payment method
3. Check failed payment reason
4. Escalate if repeated or affecting multiple members

---

## WALLET / BALANCE

Use wallet/balance to check:

- Credit
- Debit
- Applied payments
- Available balance
- Allocation

If wallet balance does not match expectation:

1. Review transactions
2. Check bill allocation
3. Check previous credits/debits
4. Escalate if mismatch remains

---

## INACTIVE MEMBERS

Inactive members may have limited access or payment issues.

Check:

- Member status
- Subscription status
- Payment scheme status
- Outstanding bills

Do not reactivate or change status unless confirmed.

---

## REMOVE OR CANCEL SUBSCRIPTION

Always confirm before removing or cancelling a subscription.

Before changing:

1. Confirm member
2. Confirm subscription
3. Confirm effective date
4. Check billing impact

Escalate if cancellation affects billing history or payment schedules.

---

## COMMON CAUSE → EFFECT

- Unpublished bill means member cannot see it
- Unallocated payment may make bill appear unpaid
- Inactive member may affect payment schemes
- Subscription setup controls recurring bill amount
- Discounts may affect final bill total
- Previous unpaid balance may increase current bill

---

## ESCALATION TRIGGERS

Escalate if:

- Bill total does not match after checks
- Payment exists but cannot be allocated
- Payment scheme repeatedly fails
- Wallet/balance mismatch remains
- Member dispute cannot be resolved
- Billing history appears inconsistent
- Multiple members are affected

---

## RESPONSE STYLE

Preferred format:

1. Acknowledge briefly
2. Identify the bill/member issue
3. Give exact area to check
4. Ask one follow-up if needed
