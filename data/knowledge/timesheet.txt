BRS KNOWLEDGE FILE: TIMESHEET

Use this file for questions about the Timesheet, tee sheet, tee times, bookings, booking details, reservation types, booking statuses, in-grid booking, printing/exporting the timesheet, competitions shown on the tee sheet, and online booking behaviour that affects tee times.

Source basis:
- Public BRS Help Center: Timesheet and Bookings sections, especially Configure the timesheet and Add a single tee time booking.
- Approved system crawl: Timesheet day view, reservation statuses, online booking surfaces, and multi-course observations.
- Built-in system help and field labels observed in the BRS demo/system crawl.

Support style:
- Use "timesheet" and "tee sheet" as equivalent BRS terms when helping users.
- Give concrete navigation where known.
- Do not invent a Move or Reschedule button for bookings. Approved local guidance says moving bookings should use the cut/paste workflow from Booking Details when applicable.
- Do not quote live booking names, player names, prices, references, or customer details.
- Never use internal HTML field names or database column names in customer-facing answers. Use the user-facing label instead. Examples: use "Calendar End Year" not "calendar_end_year_4"; use "the date selector" not "d_date"; use "the tee time checkboxes" not "T_ids[]"; use "Calendar Start Year" not "calendar_start_year_4".

## Main purpose

The Timesheet is the main operational area for viewing and working with tee times. It shows the club's daily tee-time rows and supports creating, editing, viewing, printing, and reporting on bookings.

## Timesheet views

BRS provides several timesheet views accessible from the Timesheet page:

- Day view (default) — shows all tee-time rows for a single date with full booking detail.
- Summary view — condensed overview of bookings for a date.
- 4 Week view — shows four weeks of tee times in a grid layout for quick scanning of availability and bookings across multiple weeks.
- Month view — calendar-style monthly overview showing which days have tee times configured and booking activity.
- Year view — full-year calendar overview showing configured days across all months.
- Both/combined course view — on multi-course systems, shows all courses together on one screen.

To switch views, use the view links above the tee-time grid on the Timesheet page (Day, Summary, 4 Week, Month, Year). Multi-course systems can also expose course-specific views and combined views. Course names are club configuration data, so only describe the multi-course pattern unless the user supplies their own course names.

## Day view structure

The day view typically includes:

- Date navigation.
- Tee-time rows.
- Hole selection.
- Reservation name.
- Reservation type.
- Player names.
- Buggy/service fields.
- Information columns.
- Print daily booking sheet link.
- Sunrise/sunset or timing markers where enabled.
- Squeeze Tee Time link where enabled.
- Add member to waiting list option where enabled.

Booking rows, player details, names, prices, and counts are live club/customer data and should not be stored or repeated as product knowledge.

## Configure the timesheet

Use Tools > Configure Timesheet for creating or changing tee time patterns.

Before setting up a new calendar year, check:

Tools > System Configuration > Calendar End Year

The Calendar End Year must extend far enough for the year the club wants to configure.

Core configure-timesheet workflow:

1. Go to Tools > Configure Timesheet.
2. Use the Configure Timesheet form at the bottom of the page, below the calendar-style year overview.
3. In Operation, choose either Configure one or multiple days with the same tee time interval or Configure one or multiple days with alternative tee time intervals.
4. If the club has more than one course or tee, use Course to choose the course being configured.
5. Select Year, Start Month, Start Day of Month, End Month, and End Day of Month.
6. Select the tee time interval or alternative intervals.
7. Select First Tee Time Hour, First Tee Time Minute, Last Tee Time Hour, and Last Tee Time Minute.
8. Choose sunrise/sunset stopping behaviour if needed.
9. Tick the weekday checkboxes under Select the days of the week you want to configure.
10. Click Configure the Timesheet.

Operation choices include:

- Configure one or multiple days with the same tee time interval.
- Configure one or multiple days with alternative tee time intervals.
- Delete selected tee times on one or multiple days.

Use same-interval configuration when the club uses one interval across the selected period. Use alternative intervals where the club alternates intervals, such as different gaps between tee times.

Configure Timesheet also exposes Timesheet Templates. Use templates when the club wants reusable timesheets that can be copied quickly from one date to another.

## Changing tee time intervals

To change the tee time interval (for example, from 10 minutes to 8 minutes):

1. Go to Tools > Configure Timesheet.
2. In "Operation", choose "Configure one or multiple days with the same tee time interval".
3. Select the "Year" and set the date range using "Start Month", "Start Day of Month", "End Month", and "End Day of Month".
4. In "Tee Time Interval", select the new interval value (for example, 8 minutes).
5. Set "First Tee Time Hour", "First Tee Time Minute", "Last Tee Time Hour", and "Last Tee Time Minute" to match the required tee time range for the day.
6. Tick the days of the week under "Select the days of the week you want to configure".
7. Click "Configure the Timesheet".
8. Check the affected dates on the Timesheet to confirm the new interval is applied.

Important notes:
- This overwrites existing tee times for the selected date range and days. Existing bookings on those tee times may be affected.
- To set different intervals for weekdays versus weekends, run Configure Timesheet twice: once for weekdays with one interval, then again for weekends with a different interval.
- To set different intervals for summer versus winter, run Configure Timesheet with different date ranges for each season.

## Adding tee times and adjusting start/end times

Timesheet interval changes belong in Configure Timesheet. The Help Center has separate guidance for changing tee time intervals, adding tee times to the start of the day, adding tee times to the end of the timesheet, and squeezing in additional tee times.

Questions about adding, removing, extending, starting earlier, finishing later, changing the first tee time, or changing the last tee time are tee-time pattern/setup questions. Use Tools > Configure Timesheet, not the single-booking workflow, unless the user is trying to book a golfer/customer/player into an existing tee time.

When answering:

- Confirm whether the user wants to change future setup or alter one specific day.
- Explain that start-of-day changes use "First Tee Time Hour" and "First Tee Time Minute", while end-of-day changes use "Last Tee Time Hour" and "Last Tee Time Minute".
- Remind the user to check "Tee Time Interval" or "Alternate Tee Time Intervals" so the spacing between tee times still matches the required setup.
- If the user only needs to insert one extra tee time on a specific day, mention the Squeeze Tee Time option on the Timesheet before advising a wider Configure Timesheet change.
- For a new year, check "Calendar End Year" first.
- For a small adjustment on a configured day, direct them to the relevant add/squeeze guidance rather than implying they must rebuild the whole year.

## Sunrise and sunset stopping behaviour

When configuring the timesheet, the sunrise/sunset stopping options control whether BRS automatically stops generating tee times before sunrise or after sunset. This is useful for clubs that want tee times to follow daylight hours.

The sunrise/sunset options appear on the Configure Timesheet form. When enabled:
- Tee times will not be generated before sunrise or after sunset for the configured date range.
- The exact sunrise/sunset times are calculated per day based on the club's geographic location settings.

If a club wants fixed start/end times regardless of season, they should leave sunrise/sunset stopping disabled and set explicit "First Tee Time" and "Last Tee Time" values instead.

## Single tee time booking

Approved Help Center workflow for adding a single 18-hole tee time booking:

1. Tick the box beside the tee time to book.
2. In the master row at the top of the page, enter reservation name, reservation type, and player names.
3. Click Add.
4. To add more information, click the time of the tee time that was booked.
5. The in-grid editor can also be used for a single tee time booking where available.

Use this when the user asks how to add a standard single tee time booking. If they need contact details, payments, refunds, or transaction history, route to the more specific booking details or payments guidance.

## Cancel or delete a booking

There are two separate operations that users may describe as "deleting" on the Timesheet. It is important to distinguish between them:

### Delete a booking (remove a reservation from a tee time)

Use this when the user wants to cancel or remove a booking that has been made on an existing tee time. The tee time slot itself remains; only the reservation is removed.

1. Open the Timesheet from the main navigation menu.
2. Navigate to the date of the booking.
3. Tick the checkbox beside the booked tee time you want to cancel.
4. Click "Delete" in the action toolbar above the tee-time grid.
5. The reservation is removed and the tee time becomes available again.

Important:
- If the booking has payments attached via BRS Payments, check whether a refund is required before deleting.
- Deleted bookings may still appear in the Cancelled Bookings report under Reports.
- For block bookings, select all the tee times in the block before clicking Delete, or use the block booking management tools.

### Delete tee time slots (remove time slots from the timesheet)

Use this when the user wants to remove tee time slots entirely from a date range — for example, removing all tee times on a closure day or shortening the day.

1. Go to Tools > Configure Timesheet.
2. In "Operation", choose "Delete selected tee times on one or multiple days".
3. Select the "Year" and date range.
4. Set the time range and days of week to target.
5. Click "Configure the Timesheet".

This removes the tee time slots themselves. Any bookings on those slots will also be removed.

Do not confuse these two operations when answering:
- "Delete a booking" or "cancel a booking" = remove the reservation from the Timesheet grid.
- "Delete tee times" or "remove tee time slots" = Configure Timesheet > Delete operation.

## Add a player to an existing booking

To add a player to a booking that has already been made:

1. Open the Timesheet from the main navigation menu.
2. Navigate to the date and find the booked tee time.
3. Click the time of the booked tee time to open Booking Details.
4. In Booking Details, add the player name in the next available player field.
5. Click "Save" or "Update" to confirm the change.

If the tee time has available player slots, the additional player name will appear in the tee-time grid after saving. If all player slots are full, consider whether the booking needs to be expanded or an additional tee time booked.

## Remove a player from a booking

To remove a player from an existing booking:

1. Open the Timesheet from the main navigation menu.
2. Navigate to the date and find the booked tee time.
3. Click the time of the booked tee time to open Booking Details.
4. In Booking Details, clear the player name from the relevant player field.
5. Click "Save" or "Update" to confirm the change.

The player will be removed from the tee-time display. If removing the last player from a booking, consider whether the entire booking should be deleted instead.

## Block bookings and repeated bookings

The Bookings Help Center section includes block booking workflows and bookings over multiple weeks. Use the general rule:

- A single tee time booking is one selected tee time.
- A block booking is used for multiple tee times or repeated reservations.
- Confirm date, time range, reservation type, and repetition before giving procedural advice.

Do not store or repeat real booking names, group names, or customer details.

## Booking details

Clicking the booked tee time opens the booking details. Booking details is the safer place for:

- Adding more information to a booking.
- Adding contact details.
- Viewing transaction history.
- Handling payments/refunds where BRS Payments is involved.
- Moving/cutting/pasting booking content where the approved process applies.

If the user asks about payment on a booking, confirm whether it was taken through BRS Payments before advising on refunds.

## Messages on the Timesheet

"Messages on the Timesheet" is a feature that displays text messages directly on the tee sheet for members and/or visitors to see when they view a particular date. This is NOT the same as emailing the timesheet.

To add or edit a message on the timesheet:

1. Go to Tools > Messages on the Timesheet (in the Additional Set Up Requirements section).
2. Use "Select a Year" if the message is for a different year.
3. Set the "Start Date" and "End Date" for when the message should appear.
4. Enter the message text in the "Message on the Timesheet" field.
5. Set the "Message Time" if the message should appear at a specific tee time position.
6. Select the "Days of Week" the message should display on.
7. Tick "Members" and/or "Visitors" to control who sees the message.
8. Click "Save" or "Add" to apply the message.

Common uses:
- Course closed notices (e.g., "Course closed for maintenance").
- Competition notices (e.g., "Ladies Competition — 1st tee reserved 9:00–11:00").
- Seasonal messages (e.g., "Winter mats in use").
- Event reminders displayed directly on the booking sheet.

Important disambiguation:
- "Messages on the Timesheet" = text displayed on the tee sheet view for specific dates. Found at Tools > Messages on the Timesheet.
- "Email the Timesheet" = sending an email to members/contacts about bookings on a date. Found at Tools > Email Messaging > Email the Timesheet.

When a user asks about "adding a message to the timesheet", they almost always mean the display message (Messages on the Timesheet), not the email function.

## Timesheet Templates

Timesheet Templates allow clubs to save a reusable timesheet configuration that can be applied quickly to different dates without re-entering all the settings manually each time.

Timesheet Templates are accessed from the Configure Timesheet page at Tools > Configure Timesheet. The "Timesheet Templates" link appears on that page.

Use templates when:
- The club has a standard setup they apply repeatedly (e.g., summer weekday pattern, winter weekend pattern).
- The club wants to quickly copy a proven configuration to a new date range.
- Different seasons or event types require switching between pre-saved configurations.

Note: Detailed template creation and application steps depend on the club's BRS version. Direct the user to the Timesheet Templates link on the Configure Timesheet page and the BRS Help Center article on Timesheet Templates for the most current instructions.

## Copy Timesheet

BRS does not have a direct "copy timesheet from one week to another" button on the main Timesheet grid. To replicate a timesheet setup across multiple weeks or periods:

Option 1 — Use Configure Timesheet with the same settings:
1. Go to Tools > Configure Timesheet.
2. Apply the same configuration (interval, times, days) to the new date range.

Option 2 — Use Timesheet Templates:
1. If the club has saved a Timesheet Template, apply it to the target date range from the Configure Timesheet page.

Option 3 — Copy individual bookings:
- To copy bookings (not the tee time structure), use the Copy and Paste functions in the Timesheet action toolbar. This copies reservation content between tee times, not the underlying tee time setup.

Clarify with the user whether they want to:
- Copy the tee time structure/intervals (use Configure Timesheet or Templates).
- Copy actual bookings/reservations from one date to another (use Copy/Paste on the Timesheet).

## Printing and exporting

The Timesheet Help Center includes guidance for printing or exporting a timesheet. The day view can include a Print Daily Booking Sheet action.

When a user asks for a print/export:

- Confirm the date or range.
- Confirm whether they need the day sheet, booking report, or a report export.
- If the request is analytical or date-range based, use Reports rather than the day sheet.

## Competitions on the timesheet

Member competitions and open competitions interact with the timesheet, while setup belongs under Tools or competition-specific pages.

Use this distinction:

- Member competitions generally involve member booking, draws, competition purse, and member entry setup.
- Open competitions are visitor/public-facing competitions with their own rates and entry flow.
- For competition tee times not showing online, check competition setup, release rules, booking rules, and online availability configuration.

If the question is about charging members, use competition purse guidance. If it is about visitor/open competition charges, use open competition/visitor fee setup.

## Online booking surfaces

BRS exposes separate online booking surfaces:

- Member Booking for members signing in, registering, or recovering credentials.
- Visitor Booking for public visitor tee times.
- Tee Time Availability for availability views.
- Tour operator login where enabled.
- Open Competitions for visitor/public competition booking.

Online booking content such as visitor terms, live rates, messages, phone numbers, availability, and competition lists is club-specific.

## Common routing

- Tee time creation, row display, booking details, reservation types: Timesheet.
- Green fee rate setup: Tools or Visitor Booking depending on manual versus online rate.
- Booking refund: Payments if BRS Payments is involved; otherwise follow non-BRS payment process or escalate.
- Member unable to book online: Member Booking/Memberships plus Tools > Member Casual Booking Rules.
- Staff cannot access the timesheet: Users/permissions.
