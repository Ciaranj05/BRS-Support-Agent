# TEESHEET – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

Use this knowledge for tee times, bookings, players, cancellations, moving bookings, booking visibility, payment status on bookings, and tee sheet behaviour.

---

## CORE RULE

If a booking cannot be found, always confirm:

- Date
- Time

Do this before checking payments.

---

## OPEN A BOOKING

To open a booking:

1. Go to the tee sheet
2. Select the relevant date
3. Click the tee time
4. Open the booking details

Use the booking details to check:

- Players
- Booking status
- Payments
- Notes
- Source/channel
- Booking history where available

---

## BOOKING NOT FOUND

If the user cannot find a booking:

Ask:

Can you confirm the booking date and tee time?

Then check:

1. Correct date
2. Correct course/tee sheet if applicable
3. Time of booking
4. Player/customer name
5. Whether booking was moved or cancelled

If payment is mentioned, still confirm date/time first.

Escalate if:

- Booking should exist but cannot be located
- Multiple bookings are missing
- Booking history is inconsistent

---

## CANCEL A BOOKING

Before cancelling, confirm the user intends to cancel.

Steps:

1. Open the tee time
2. Open the booking
3. Confirm the booking/customer
4. Check payment status
5. Cancel the booking

---

## MOVE A BOOKING

When a user asks how to move, transfer, or reschedule a booking, do not ask whether it is for the same day or a different day. The process is the same either way.

Before moving, confirm:

- Original date/time
- New date/time

To move a booking and keep payment information attached:

1. Click the tee time to open the booking details page
2. Click Cut from inside the booking details page
3. Return to the tee sheet
4. Go to the new date if the booking is being moved to a different day
5. Tick the checkbox beside the tee time you want to move the booking to
6. Click Paste

Important:

- Do not tell users to use a Move or Reschedule option for teesheet bookings
- Do not tell users to right-click the booking to move it
- Do not tell users to drag and drop or click a slot to drop the booking
- Using Cut from inside the booking keeps the payment information attached
- If you use the checkbox on the tee sheet first and cut the booking from there, the payment information will not transfer
- Always check the payment status after moving the booking
