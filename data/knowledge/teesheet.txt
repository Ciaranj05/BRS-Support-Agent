# TEESHEET – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

Use this knowledge for tee times, bookings, players, cancellations, moving bookings, booking visibility, payment status on bookings, and tee sheet behaviour.

---

## CORE RULE

If a booking cannot be found, always confirm:

- Date
- Time

Do this before checking payments.

---

## OPEN A BOOKING

To open a booking:

1. Go to the tee sheet
2. Select the relevant date
3. Click the tee time
4. Open the booking details

Use the booking details to check:

- Players
- Booking status
- Payments
- Notes
- Source/channel
- Booking history where available

---

## BOOKING NOT FOUND

If the user cannot find a booking:

Ask:

“Can you confirm the booking date and tee time?”

Then check:

1. Correct date
2. Correct course/tee sheet if applicable
3. Time of booking
4. Player/customer name
5. Whether booking was moved or cancelled

If payment is mentioned, still confirm date/time first.

Escalate if:

- Booking should exist but cannot be located
- Multiple bookings are missing
- Booking history is inconsistent

---

## CANCEL A BOOKING

Before cancelling, confirm the user intends to cancel.

Steps:

1. Open the tee time
2. Open the booking
3. Confirm the booking/customer
4. Check payment status
5. Cancel the booking

Important:

- Always confirm before cancellation
- Check whether refund action is needed
- If payment exists, follow payments/refund logic

Escalate if cancellation fails or payment/refund status is unclear.

---

## REFUND A BOOKING

Before processing a refund, confirm:

- Correct booking/customer
- Refund amount
- Whether the refund should be full or partial
- Whether the booking should also be cancelled

Steps:

1. Open the tee time
2. Open the booking details
3. Expand or open the Payments tab/section
4. Review payment status and payment history
5. Select the refund option or refund button
6. Choose full refund or partial refund amount if applicable
7. Confirm the refund
8. Verify the payment status updates correctly after refund

Important:

- The refund option is normally inside the Payments section of the booking
- Some systems may show "Cancel and Refund" instead of a separate refund button
- Check for overpayments or remaining balances after partial refunds
- If refund approval is required, follow the club approval process

Escalate if:

- Refund button is missing
- Payment record does not match booking status
- Refund fails or remains pending
- The refunded amount looks incorrect

---

## MOVE A BOOKING

Before moving, confirm:

- Original date/time
- New date/time
- Number of players
- Any price difference

Steps:

1. Open the existing booking
2. Choose move/change booking option
3. Select new tee time
4. Confirm player details
5. Check payment status after move

Important:

- Moving a booking may change payment totals
- Moving may create part-paid or overpaid status
- Check payment allocation after moving

---

## ADD PLAYER TO BOOKING

Before adding a player, check:

- Tee time capacity
- Booking rules
- Member/visitor restrictions
- Price impact

Steps:

1. Open booking
2. Add player
3. Confirm player type
4. Save booking
5. Check payment amount if required

---

## REMOVE PLAYER FROM BOOKING

Before removing a player, confirm the change.

Steps:

1. Open booking
2. Remove player
3. Save booking
4. Check payment status

Important:

- Removing a player may create an overpayment
- If payment exists, check whether refund is needed

---

## PAYMENT STATUS ON BOOKING

Booking colours:

- Green = Fully paid
- Blue = Part paid
- Red = Unpaid
- Yellow = Overpaid

If payment status looks wrong:

1. Open booking
2. Go to Payments tab
3. Check payment total
4. Check allocation
5. If payment exists but status is wrong, use payments knowledge

---

## BOOKING VISIBILITY

If a tee time or booking is not visible online, check:

- Date/time availability
- Booking rules
- Member/visitor restrictions
- Blocked times
- Competition/society reservations
- Online booking settings

Escalate if the tee time should be visible but is not.

---

## PLAYER DETAILS

Use booking details to check:

- Player names
- Contact details
- Member/visitor status
- Notes
- Booking source

If details are wrong, confirm the correct details before updating.

---

## SOCIETY OR GROUP BOOKINGS

For society/group bookings, check:

- Number of players
- Tee time range
- Deposit/payment status
- Contact details
- Booking notes

If payment is involved, follow payments logic.

---

## COMMON CAUSE → EFFECT

- Removing players may create overpayment
- Moving booking may change price
- Booking rules may hide tee times
- Blocked times may prevent booking
- Incorrect date/time search may make booking appear missing
- Payment allocation may cause incorrect payment status

---

## ESCALATION TRIGGERS

Escalate if:

- Booking cannot be found after date/time checks
- Booking data appears inconsistent
- Booking cannot be moved/cancelled
- Payment status does not match payment record
- Multiple bookings are affected
- Tee sheet availability does not match setup

---

## RESPONSE STYLE

Keep tee sheet answers direct.

Preferred format:

1. Acknowledge briefly
2. Confirm date/time if booking is missing
3. Give exact next check
4. Ask one follow-up if needed
