BRS KNOWLEDGE FILE: CONTACTS

Use this file for non-member contact records, visitor/contact databases, companies, hotels, societies, tour operators, contact categories, and communication/reporting involving contacts.

Source basis:
- Approved system crawl: Contacts page and contact category observations.
- Public BRS Help Center: Communication via Email and Text, Visitor Booking, Reporting, and Basic Set Up section listings.
- Tools index observations for Contact Categories.

Support style:
- Distinguish Contacts from Users and Members.
- Do not store or repeat real contact names, emails, phone numbers, postcodes, or customer records.
- For member records, use Memberships/Users depending on the task.

## Purpose

Contacts stores non-member contact records used for booking, reporting, and messaging workflows.

Typical contact audiences include:

- Visitors.
- Societies.
- Tour operators.
- Hotels.
- Companies.
- Other non-member contacts.

## Contacts page

Observed Contacts actions include:

- View Contacts.
- Add New.
- View All.
- Filter by category.

Observed fields include:

- Search text.
- Contact category.

Category counts and actual contact records are club data and must not be treated as reusable product knowledge.

## Contact categories

Contact Categories are configured from Tools. They classify non-member contact records for searching, reporting, and messaging.

Observed category concepts include:

- Company.
- Hotel.
- Other.
- Society.
- Tour Operator.
- Visitor.
- Not set.

Clubs may have their own contact categories. Ask the user what category they need if the answer depends on local setup.

## Add or find a contact

Use Contacts > Add New when a user wants to create a non-member contact record.

On Add Contact, observed/approved fields include:

- General Information, including Company / Group Name for organisations such as tour operators, hotels, societies, and companies.
- Contact Category, such as Visitor, Society, Tour Operator, Hotel, Company, Other, or a club custom category.
- Contact fields for title, first name, last name, email, telephone, and mobile where supplied.
- Address Information for address, town/city, county/state, country, and postcode/zip.
- Club Details for club name, handicap, and CDH where those golf details are relevant.
- Marketing Preferences where the contact has given permission.

When a club says a contact has disappeared and asks for instructions, first carry forward the missing contact context. If they want to recreate it, use Contacts > Add New, set the company/group name and category, save, then return to View All/search/category filters to confirm the expected count. If the old record later reappears, check which record has booking or revenue history before deleting, merging, or replacing anything.

Use Contacts search/filter controls when the user wants to find an existing non-member contact. Search can be paired with categories to narrow results.

For a person who should be able to log in as a member, use Users or Memberships rather than Contacts.

## Contacts and bookings

Contacts can be linked to visitor, society, company, hotel, tour operator, or other non-member booking workflows.

If the user asks about adding contact details to a booking:

- Use the Booking Details route from the Timesheet/Bookings area.
- Use Contacts if they want to manage the reusable contact record itself.

## Contacts and messaging

Email Contacts is used for contacting visitors, societies, and similar contact database records.

SMS guidance also includes sending text messages to Contacts / Visitors where SMS is enabled.

Before advising on bulk communication:

- Confirm whether the audience is members or contacts.
- Confirm whether they want email, SMS, or app/club message.
- Confirm whether contact consent/communication preferences need to be respected.

## Contacts and reports

Reports can include contact details, contact email address reports, revenue by customer contact, and revenue by tour operator or hotel depending on setup.

Use Reports for exporting or analysing contact data. Use Contacts for maintaining individual records.

## Related areas

- Timesheet/Booking Details for booking contact details.
- Visitor Booking for visitor-facing bookings.
- Messages for email/SMS to contacts.
- Tools for contact category setup.
- Reports for contact exports and contact revenue reporting.
