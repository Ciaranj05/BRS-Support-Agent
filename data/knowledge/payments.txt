# PAYMENTS – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

Use this knowledge when the user asks about payments, refunds, payouts, VAT, payment requests, failed payments, missing payments, or payment allocation.

---

## CORE PAYMENT RULES

Always check BRS first.

Primary checks:

1. Booking / bill
2. Payments tab
3. Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions

Do not assume payment method.
Do not manually mark payments as paid unless the approved process confirms it.
If BRS shows no record but the user confirms money was taken, escalate.

---

## ACCESS PAYMENTS

Go to:

Tools >> BRS Payments

Main areas:

- Transactions
- Payouts
- VAT Reports
- General Payment Requests

---

## TRANSACTIONS

Go to:

Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions

Use this to:

- View all transactions
- Search by amount
- Search by date
- Check payment status
- Identify refunds
- Confirm whether payment exists in BRS

When checking a missing payment, always search using:

- Date
- Approximate time if available
- Amount
- Customer / golfer name if available
- Booking or bill reference if available

---

## PAYOUTS

Go to:

Tools >> BRS Payments >> Payouts

Use this to check:

- When funds are paid into the bank account
- Payout breakdowns
- Which transactions are included in a payout

Important:

- Payouts follow a rolling schedule, typically around 7 days
- Payout totals may differ from gross sales because fees are deducted
- Payouts include transaction fees

If payout total does not match expected amount:

1. Check payout breakdown
2. Compare transactions included
3. Check processing fees
4. Escalate if there is a reporting discrepancy

---

## VAT REPORTS

Go to:

Tools >> BRS Payments >> VAT Reports

Use this to:

- View monthly breakdowns
- Download CSV or PDF reports

Reports include:

- Gross sales
- Processing fees
- Net sales
- VAT breakdown

Escalate if figures do not match after checking transactions and payout breakdowns.

---

## GENERAL PAYMENT REQUESTS

Used for non-booking payments.

Go to:

Tools >> BRS Payments >> General Payment Requests

Steps:

1. Click Create Payment Request
2. Enter the required details
3. Send the payment request

Used for:

- Membership fees
- Shop items
- Miscellaneous charges
- Non-booking payments

---

## BOOKING PAYMENT REQUESTS

Used when requesting payment for a booking.

Steps:

1. Click the tee time
2. Open the booking
3. Click Payments
4. Click New Booking Payment
5. Send the payment link

Used for:

- Deposits
- Outstanding balances
- Booking payments

---

## TAKE PAYMENT

Steps:

1. Click the tee time
2. Open the booking
3. Click Payments
4. Click Pay Now
5. Enter card details
6. Confirm payment

---

## VIRTUAL TERMINAL

Used for taking payment over the phone.

Requires:

- Card number
- Expiry date
- CVC
- Billing details

If payment fails:

1. Check card details
2. Retry payment
3. Ask customer to try another card or method
4. Escalate if repeated failures occur or multiple users are affected

---

## REFUNDS

Before refunding, always confirm the correct payment.

Steps:

1. Click the tee time
2. Open the booking
3. Click Payments
4. Find the correct payment
5. Click Refund
6. Choose full or partial refund
7. Confirm

Important:

- Refunds can be full or partial
- Refunds must be processed against the correct payment
- Confirm before processing refunds

---

## ONLINE REFUNDS

Online refunds:

- Return to the original card
- Can take 5–10 days
- Are automatically recorded in BRS

If customer says refund has not arrived:

1. Confirm refund exists in BRS
2. Confirm refund date
3. Advise expected refund timeframe
4. Escalate if outside normal timeframe

---

## OFFLINE REFUNDS

Offline refunds include:

- Cash
- PDQ
- Cheque

Important:

- These must be completed outside BRS
- BRS only records the refund
- Do not imply money has been returned unless the offline refund was actually completed

---

## PAYMENT STATUS

Booking colours:

- Green = Fully paid
- Blue = Part paid
- Red = Unpaid
- Yellow = Overpaid

If colour does not match expectation:

1. Open the booking
2. Check Payments tab
3. Check allocation
4. Check left to allocate

---

## PAYMENT ALLOCATION

Payments may exist but not be allocated correctly.

Check:

- Payment exists
- Payment is linked to correct booking / bill
- “Left to Allocate” = 0

If payment exists but booking or bill still appears unpaid:

1. Check allocation
2. Confirm whether payment is applied to the right item
3. Correct allocation if process allows
4. Escalate if mismatch cannot be resolved

---

## OVERPAYMENTS

Common causes:

- Removing players
- Changing booking price
- Moving bookings
- Applying payment to wrong item

Action:

1. Review payment
2. Check amount paid versus amount due
3. Check allocation
4. Process refund if required and confirmed

Do not refund until the correct payment and overpayment amount are confirmed.

---

## PAYMENT FAILURES

Common causes:

- Incorrect card details
- Expired card
- Insufficient funds
- Bank rejection
- Stripe rejection

Steps:

1. Retry payment
2. Check card details
3. Try alternative method
4. Escalate if multiple users are affected
5. Escalate if the same error repeats after normal checks

---

## PAYMENT NOT SHOWING / CUSTOMER SAYS THEY PAID

This is a critical scenario.

System-first checks:

1. Open the booking or bill
2. Check the Payments tab
3. Go to:
   Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions
4. Search by:
   - Amount
   - Date
   - Customer / golfer name
   - Booking or bill reference

---

### OUTCOME A — PAYMENT EXISTS IN BRS

If payment exists in BRS:

Possible issue:

- Payment mismatch
- Allocation issue
- Payment linked to wrong item
- Booking / bill status not updating correctly

Next step:

1. Check allocation
2. Check booking / bill balance
3. Check whether “Left to Allocate” is 0
4. Escalate if mismatch remains

---

### OUTCOME B — NO PAYMENT IN BRS, BUT USER SAYS MONEY WAS TAKEN

If no payment exists in BRS and the user confirms money was taken:

Escalate immediately.

Do not:

- Ask for further proof
- Assume bank transfer
- Manually mark as paid
- Continue troubleshooting
- Tell the user to wait without escalation

Use this wording:

“I understand — that’s frustrating.

Since there’s no record in BRS, this needs to be checked with the payments platform.

I can raise this with support now.”

---

### OUTCOME C — NO PAYMENT IN BRS AND USER IS NOT SURE MONEY WAS TAKEN

If no payment exists and the user is unsure whether money was taken:

Ask one question:

“Can the customer confirm whether money has actually left their account?”

Then:

- If yes → escalate
- If no → advise retrying payment or checking payment method
- If unsure → ask them to confirm with their bank/card statement

---

## MEMBERSHIP PAYMENT ISSUES

### Payment not applied to bill

Steps:

1. Open member profile
2. Go to Billing
3. Open the bill
4. Check payment allocation
5. Confirm whether payment exists but is unallocated

Important:

- Payment may exist but not be allocated
- Do not assume payment is missing until allocation is checked

---

### Bill not matching expectation

Ask:

“What looks incorrect — amount too high, missing payment, or extra charge?”

Then check:

Memberships >> Billing / Payments

Review:

- Subscriptions
- Custom charges
- Discounts
- Payments
- Allocation

---

### Bill too high

Check:

- Subscriptions applied
- Custom charges
- Discounts
- Previous unpaid balance
- Payment allocation

---

### Bill not visible

Check:

- Bill is published
- Member has access
- Correct member profile is being viewed

---

## CAUSE → EFFECT

- Removing players may create overpayment
- Moving bookings may change payment totals
- Inactive member may cause payment failure
- Unpublished bill means member cannot see it
- Unallocated payment may make a bill appear unpaid
- Fees can make payout amount lower than gross sales

---

## ESCALATION TRIGGERS

Escalate if:

- Payment mismatch remains after checks
- No payment in BRS but money was taken
- Refund is unclear
- Multiple payment failures occur
- Reporting figures do not match after checks
- Customer disputes payment
- Payment provider investigation is required

---

## RESPONSE STYLE FOR PAYMENT ISSUES

Keep payment answers operational.

Preferred format:

1. Acknowledge briefly
2. Give the next check
3. Ask one follow-up if needed

Example:

“Got it — let’s check the payment record first.

Please go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions

Search by the payment amount and date.

Can you see a matching transaction there?”

---

## KEY BEHAVIOUR

Always:

- Check system first
- Confirm payment status
- Check allocation before assuming payment is missing
- Escalate when escalation conditions are met

Never:

- Assume external payment method
- Manually mark as paid without confirmation
- Continue troubleshooting after escalation condition is met
- Ask for proof when payment has already been confirmed and no BRS record exists
