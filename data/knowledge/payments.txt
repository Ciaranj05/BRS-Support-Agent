BRS KNOWLEDGE FILE: PAYMENTS

Use this file for BRS Payments, booking payment status, transactions, payouts, refunds, payment requests, merchant payment reports, and card-payment reconciliation.

Source basis:
- Approved BRS demo-system crawl: BRS Payments, Transactions, Payouts, and Reports payment options.
- Approved Reports knowledge: payment/date/report categories.
- Existing support guidance for separating booking payments from membership bill payments.

Support style:
- Do not say a customer's payment succeeded, failed, paid out, or was refunded unless live payment evidence proves it.
- Do not process or advise processing refunds until the booking/payment record and transaction status have been checked.
- Keep booking/card payments separate from membership bill payments unless the user explicitly says the payment is for a membership bill.

Priority answer cues:
- For a booking where the Timesheet shows balance outstanding but the customer says card payment was taken, do not rely on the balance alone. Open the tee time/booking details, expand/check the Payments section, then cross-check Tools > BRS Payments > Transactions using the booking reference, customer name, date, amount, and status.
- Use Tools > BRS Payments > Payouts to check whether a completed transaction is expected in a payout schedule. Use Refunds only after the original transaction is confirmed and the refund route is correct.
- If there is no completed BRS Payments transaction, explain that BRS cannot confirm funds or process a BRS refund from the booking until the successful transaction is found. Escalate with the booking reference, booking date/time, transaction search result, and payout evidence.
- For tournament/open competition reconciliation where withdrawn entries are missing from the tee sheet, use BRS Payments Transactions and payment/revenue reports as evidence alongside the competition entry list; the tee sheet alone will not prove all paid entrants.

## Main purpose

BRS Payments is used when staff need to check card transactions, refunds, payment requests, payouts, VAT/payment exports, or online merchant payment reconciliation.

Use payment evidence when the user mentions:

- Card payment.
- Debit card.
- Transaction.
- Refund.
- Payout.
- Payout schedule.
- Payment request.
- Payment successful or unsuccessful.
- Balance outstanding after a customer says they paid.
- Online payment reconciliation.

## Booking payment discrepancy

When a booking shows an outstanding balance but the customer says payment was taken:

1. Find the booking by booking reference, name, date, time, or Timesheet row.
2. Open Booking Details.
3. Check the Payments section in Booking Details for balance, payment status, method, amount, and refund status.
4. Open Tools > BRS Payments > Transactions.
5. Search/filter Transactions by booking reference, name, date, amount, or payment status.
6. Confirm whether the card transaction succeeded, failed, was refunded, or is absent.
7. If the transaction succeeded but the booking still shows outstanding, escalate with the booking reference, transaction status, amount, date/time, and screenshots.

Do not treat the Timesheet balance alone as proof that a card payment failed. Use Booking Details and Transactions together.

## Payout checks

Use Payouts when the club asks why money has not appeared in the payout schedule or bank payout.

Observed Payouts fields/columns include:

- Start Date.
- End Date.
- Amount.
- Transfer Date.
- Status.

Suggested checks:

1. Open Tools > BRS Payments > Payouts.
2. Set Start Date and End Date to cover the expected payout window.
3. Compare Amount, Transfer Date, and Status with the successful Transactions for the booking/event.
4. If a successful transaction is missing from the expected payout, escalate with the booking/event reference, transaction details, payout date range, amount, and payout status.

## Transaction checks

Use Transactions when the club needs the payment-level record.

Transactions is the best place to confirm:

- Whether a card payment exists.
- Whether it succeeded or failed.
- Whether a refund exists.
- Which booking/event/reference the payment belongs to.
- Whether the payment can be compared against payout records.

## Payment and revenue reports

The main Reports area includes payment-related report options that can support reconciliation:

- Revenue From Online Merchant Payments.
- Payments by Payment Date.
- Payments by Transaction Date/Time & User.
- Booking / Payment Status.
- Booking Transactions by Date Booked.
- Booking Transactions by Date Playing.

Use Reports when the club needs totals, date ranges, exports, or printed output. Use Tools > BRS Payments when the club needs the payment processor transaction or payout record.

## Refunds

Before advising a refund:

1. Confirm the booking or bill object.
2. Confirm the original payment status and method.
3. Confirm the amount and whether a previous refund already exists.
4. Use BRS Payments refund records for BRS/card payments.
5. Escalate if the payment status is unclear or the refund would affect a disputed payment.

Membership bill refunds should use Memberships billing/payment evidence first. Booking refunds should use Booking Details and BRS Payments evidence.
