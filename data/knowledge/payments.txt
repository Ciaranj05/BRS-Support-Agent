# PAYMENTS – SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE

---

## ACCESS PAYMENTS

Go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments

---

## TRANSACTIONS

Go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions

Use this to:

• View all transactions  
• Search by amount or date  
• Check payment details  
• Identify refunds  

---

## PAYOUTS

Go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments >> Payouts

Shows:

• When funds are paid into your bank account  
• Payout breakdowns  

Important:

• Payouts are on a rolling schedule (typically ~7 days)  
• Include transaction fees  

---

## VAT REPORTS

Go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments >> VAT Reports

Use this to:

• View monthly breakdowns  
• Download CSV or PDF  

Includes:

• Gross sales  
• Processing fees  
• Net sales  
• VAT breakdown  

---

## GENERAL PAYMENT REQUESTS

Go to:
Tools >> BRS Payments >> General Payment Requests

1. Click Create Payment Request  
2. Enter details  
3. Send  

Used for:

• Membership fees  
• Shop items  
• Miscellaneous charges  

---

## BOOKING PAYMENT REQUESTS

Used when requesting payment for a booking.

1. Click the tee time  
2. Open booking  
3. Click Payments  
4. Click New Booking Payment  
5. Send payment link  

Used for:

• Deposits  
• Outstanding balances  

---

## TAKE PAYMENT

1. Click the tee time  
2. Open booking  
3. Click Payments  
4. Click Pay Now  
5. Enter card details  
6. Confirm  

---

## VIRTUAL TERMINAL

Used for taking payment over the phone.

Requires:

• Card number  
• Expiry date  
• CVC  
• Billing details  

---

## REFUNDS

To process a refund:

1. Click the tee time  
2. Open booking  
3. Click Payments  
4. Find the payment  
5. Click Refund  
6. Confirm  

Important:

• Refunds can be full or partial  
• Must be processed against the correct payment  

---

### Online refunds

• Returned to original card  
• Can take 5–10 days  
• Automatically recorded  

---

### Offline refunds (cash / PDQ / cheque)

• Must be completed outside BRS  
• System only records the refund  

---

## PAYMENT STATUS

Booking colours:

• Green = Fully paid  
• Blue = Part paid  
• Red = Unpaid  
• Yellow = Overpaid  

---

## PAYMENT ALLOCATION

Payments may be split across items.

Check:

• “Left to Allocate” = 0  

Important:

• Payments may exist but not be allocated correctly  
• Partial payments can be applied to individual items  

---

## OVERPAYMENTS

Common causes:

• Removing players  
• Changing booking price  
• Moving bookings  

Action:

• Review payment  
• Process refund if required  

---

## PAYMENT FAILURES

Common causes:

• Incorrect card details  
• Expired card  
• Insufficient funds  
• Bank rejection  
• Stripe rejection  

Steps:

1. Retry payment  
2. Check details  
3. Try alternative method  

IF multiple users affected:
→ Escalate  

---

## PAYMENT NOT SHOWING / CUSTOMER SAYS THEY PAID

System-first checks:

1. Click tee time → open booking → Payments  
2. Go to:
   Tools >> BRS Payments >> Transactions  
3. Search by amount/date  

---

### Outcomes

IF payment exists in BRS:

→ Possible mismatch  
→ Escalate  

---

IF NO payment in BRS AND money has been received:

→ Escalate immediately  

Important:

• Do NOT:
  - Assume bank transfer  
  - Manually mark as paid  
  - Continue troubleshooting  

• This must be checked with the payment provider  

---

## COMMON PAYMENT ISSUES

---

### Payment not applied to bill (Memberships)

1. Open member profile  
2. Go to Billing  
3. Open bill  
4. Check payment allocation  

Important:

• Payment may exist but not be allocated  

---

### Bill not matching expectation

Ask:

→ “What looks incorrect — amount too high, missing payment, or extra charge?”

Then check:

Memberships >> Billing / Payments

Review:

• Subscriptions  
• Custom charges  
• Discounts  

---

### Bill too high

Check:

• Subscriptions applied  
• Charges  
• Discounts  

---

### Bill not visible

Check:

• Bill is published  

---

## CAUSE → EFFECT

• Removing players → may create overpayment  
• Moving bookings → may change payment totals  
• Inactive member → payments may fail  
• Unpublished bill → member cannot see it  

---

## ESCALATION TRIGGERS

Escalate if:

• Payment mismatch  
• No payment in BRS but money received  
• Refund unclear  
• Multiple payment failures  
• Reporting discrepancies  
• Customer disputes  

---

## KEY BEHAVIOUR

Always:

✔ Check system first (booking → transactions)  
✔ Confirm payment status  
✔ Check allocation before assuming missing payment  
✔ Escalate when conditions are met  

Never:

❌ Assume external payment method  
❌ Manually mark as paid without confirmation  
❌ Continue troubleshooting after escalation condition is met
